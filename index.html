About Me

The story behind 27 years of CRM and AI leadership

I'm Nyjil Joshi — a Salesforce Senior Consultant, Enterprise Architect, and CRM & AI Thought Leader with 27+ years in the IT industry. My career has taken me from California through India, Malaysia, and across Europe, before settling in Gothenburg, Sweden.

Over the decades, I've played every role from software developer to CEO — building Salesforce practices from scratch at Dell Services and PwC, founding my own consultancy CloudUnbound Technologies, and now driving AI-augmented SDLC frameworks at Wipro for global clients in mining, automotive, and life sciences.

As an AI Generalist and CRM Specialist, I evangelize AI-infused software development and actively lead projects implementing agentic architecture, LLM frameworks, and Einstein AI across global enterprises. I hold 15 Salesforce certifications, an MBA from UBI Brussels, and an MCA from Chennai University.

Beyond technology, I'm a published author — my science fiction novel Station Nord and historical horror novel The Balkan Anomaly are both available on Amazon — and an avid thought leadership blogger.

Academic Research

Featured in Aalto University Master's Thesis on AI in CRM

Interviewed for Aalto University Research

"AI in CRM Systems: Evaluating the Prerequisites for Successful Adoption" — Roope Paju, 2020

Nyjil Joshi was interviewed as a Solution Architect at Fluido for this Aalto University School of Business Master's thesis, contributing expert insights on AI adoption in enterprise CRM systems.

The thesis investigated how companies can evaluate their readiness to adopt AI-powered CRM applications, using the fit-viability model. Nyjil's interview formed a dedicated section (Section 4.1.2.3) covering his work implementing Einstein Discovery for machine-learning-based customer churn analysis.

  • Architected ML capabilities on Salesforce using Einstein Discovery for customer churn data analysis
  • Identified prerequisites for AI-CRM: structured historical data, applicable business model, and clear initiative goals
  • Proposed expanding from churn analysis to holistic CLV, attribution, and satisfaction analysis
  • Highlighted the need to educate stakeholders on AI solutions' workings and limitations
  • Discussed AI's three roles: enhancing processes, offering novel insights, and gaining a personality

The Balkan Anomaly

Historical Horror · by Nyjil Colbridge

1730, the Habsburg frontier of Serbia. Investigator Ernst Jerybald is sent to the village of Kisiljevo to file a routine report on a string of unexplained deaths — and finds a village too frightened to bury its own dead. Following a trail of Ottoman legal records and a dead priest's journals, he uncovers a centuries-old bureaucracy built to manage something no empire will admit exists.

A second novel under the pen name Nyjil Colbridge, The Balkan Anomaly trades the far future for the archives of the past — but keeps the same preoccupation: what happens when institutions are asked to process a problem their procedures were never designed to hold.
